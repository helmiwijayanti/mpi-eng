📗 Materi 2: Langkah & bahasa procedure

1 — Kata perintah (imperative verbs)

Open, choose, tap, confirm, pay. Kalimatnya langsung menyuruh pembaca bertindak.

Contoh: “Tap SPayLater as your payment method.”

2 — Kata urutan (sequence words)

First, then, after that, next, finally. Kata ini menjaga langkah tidak tertukar.

Contoh: “First, open the app. Then, choose your item.”

3 — 7 langkah membayar pakai SPayLater

  1. First, open the app and log in.
  2. Then, choose the item and tap “Buy” / add it to the cart.
  3. After that, tap “Checkout”.
  4. Next, choose SPayLater as the payment method.
  5. Then, choose the tenor (1×, 3×, or 6×).
  6. After that, check the total and confirm with your PIN.
  7. Finally, pay each bill before its due date.

Contoh Punggur: Rani memakai langkah ini untuk sepatu futsal Rp240.000 — tenor 3× berarti Rp80.000 per bulan dalam contoh tanpa biaya admin di media ini.

Istilah sulit, dijelaskan sederhana: tenor = jumlah cicilan/bulan pembayaran. Due date = batas akhir bayar. Limit = batas maksimal yang boleh dipakai.
